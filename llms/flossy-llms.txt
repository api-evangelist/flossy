# Flossy

> Flossy builds AI agents ("AI coworkers") for dental practices, veterinary clinics and med spas: Fiona for inbound/outbound patient communication and scheduling across phone, SMS and web chat; Betsy for call-center quality scoring and marketing insight; Michael for insurance capture, benefits verification and billing; Reid for revenue forecasting and schedule/staffing optimization. FlossyHQ is the operator console and Agent Builder assembles custom agents. Founded 2020 in Santa Monica, California by Miles Beckett and Steve Seigel; began as a pay-as-you-go dental care marketplace before repositioning around agentic practice operations.
